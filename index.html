City of Bayugan · 43 barangays · ≈689 km² (688.77 km²) · pop. 109,499 (2020 Census).

    Study criteria

    Events from 1 Jan 2015 – 31 Dec 2025 with magnitude ≥ 2.0 and focal depth 0–70 km are shown. Attributes: date/time, latitude, longitude, magnitude, depth, source agency.

    Searching

    Use the search box on the Earthquakes tab. Combine anything: a place or agency (Talacogon), magnitude (M5.8, M5 = 5.0–5.9, M4+, M4-5, strong), dates (2024, Feb 2024, 10 Feb, 2024-02-10, 2020-2023), depth (shallow, deep, depth<10) and ordering (strongest, latest, nearest). Searching looks through the whole loaded catalog and ignores the filters. Click any result (or any marker) to open its details card.

    Data sources

    The app first tries the live USGS FDSN catalog. USGS is far less complete than the local network for small events (M<4), so for a full M≥2 study import your PHIVOLCS catalog export (CSV/JSON) with ⭱ Import. If the live feed is unreachable, a clearly labelled synthetic demo dataset is shown instead.

    Faults

    The Esperanza and Agusan Marsh segments are parts of the Philippine Fault in the Agusan Valley. Published estimates give roughly 50 km and 65 km length respectively. Traces drawn here are schematic, hand-placed approximations, not survey-grade. Drop an official data/faults.geojson in place to replace them.

    Documented reference events

    The demo dataset includes two events reported by PHIVOLCS in the press (M5.8 east of Esperanza, 10 Feb 2024; M5.3 Talacogon, 26 Dec 2024). Their epicenter positions and times are approximate.
